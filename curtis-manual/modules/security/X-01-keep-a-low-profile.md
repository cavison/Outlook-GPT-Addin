Reduce the chance your home becomes a target by not advertising light, noise, food or fuel during a long outage.

## You need
- Blackout curtains, blankets or cardboard for windows
- Opaque bins or tarps
- Locks for shed and garage

## Steps
1. Cover windows at night so lights inside aren't obvious from the street.
2. Run generators only when needed, with care for noise and placement; chain and lock them.
3. Keep fuel cans, supplies and empty food boxes out of sight; put trash out like everyone else's.
4. Don't post or broadcast what you have on social media or radio.
5. Cook with the smell in mind; cook when neighbors do, or share.
6. Look like the rest of the street; help neighbors so you're known as an ally, not a target.

## Caution
Low profile never means blocking ventilation or running a generator closer to the house to hide it. Generator rules (20+ feet from the house, exhaust away) always come first.

## Field note
Generators are frequently stolen during long outages. A chain through the frame to a ground anchor, plus a cheap motion light on battery, deters most thieves without drawing attention.
