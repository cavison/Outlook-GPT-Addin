Organize neighbors to watch the street and report problems, so no single household has to stay awake all night.

## You need
- Street map ([[K-02]])
- Radios ([[C-08]]) or whistles
- Notebook, pencil, flashlight
- Sign-up sheet

## Steps
1. Meet neighbors and agree that the job is observe and report, not confront.
2. Draw a simple block map with houses, entrances and who has radios or medical skills.
3. Make a watch schedule in 2-hour shifts with two people when possible.
4. Watch from a porch or window; note time, description and direction of anything unusual.
5. Report emergencies to 911 if it works, or to the neighborhood radio contact and county responders.
6. Hand over notes at shift change and rest; tired lookouts make mistakes.

## Caution
Lookouts who chase, block roads or confront people get hurt and can face criminal charges. Watch, write it down, report.

## Field note
A written description with time and direction of travel is what police can actually use later. Describe from top to bottom: hat, hair, upper clothing, lower clothing, shoes, then vehicle (color, type, plate).
