Calm a tense face-to-face situation (an upset neighbor, a stranger at the door) so nobody gets hurt.

## You need
- Nothing but practice
- A household agreement about who talks at the door

## Steps
1. Keep a safe distance and a barrier (a closed storm door, a car, a fence) between you.
2. Stand relaxed and sideways with open hands visible; don't point or crowd.
3. Speak slowly, quietly and in short sentences. Use their name if you know it.
4. Listen and repeat back what they want: "You need water for your kids."
5. Offer a choice or a next step (a resource, a time to come back), not an argument.
6. If it keeps escalating, leave, lock up and call for help; leaving is a win.

## Caution
Never open the door fully or step outside to argue. Talk through a closed door or window, and don't let pride keep you in a situation you can walk away from.

## Field note
Many people in a crisis are scared, not dangerous. Naming the feeling ("Sounds like you've had a rough couple of days") often drops the temperature faster than facts. Pre-pack a small "neighbor help" bag of water and info so you have something to offer.
