Keep one ready bag per person so you can leave home in minutes if fire, flood, gas leak or cold forces you out.

## You need
- Backpack per person
- Water and no-cook food for about 3 days
- Flashlight, radio, spare batteries, phone charger
- Medications, copies of IDs and insurance, cash in small bills
- Warm clothes, hat, gloves, rain gear, sturdy shoes
- First-aid kit, whistle, masks, hygiene items, frequency card ([[C-04]])

## Steps
1. Pack water and food for about 3 days per person (Ready.gov guidance).
2. Add a week's supply of medications and a written list of doses and doctors.
3. Put copies of IDs, insurance and key phone numbers in a waterproof bag; add small bills.
4. Pack warm layers suited to a Michigan winter, even in summer bags if you keep one year-round.
5. Add light, radio, batteries, charger, first-aid kit and whistle.
6. Keep bags by the exit; check and rotate food, water and meds every 6 months.

## Caution
A go-bag too heavy to carry gets left behind. Lift it and walk around the block; a common rule of thumb is to keep it well under a quarter of your body weight.

## Field note
Take phone photos of your documents AND print a paper copy; phones die. Put a printed contact list and a meeting-place note in every child's bag, since kids rarely know phone numbers by heart.
