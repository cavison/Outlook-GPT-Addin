Keep babies fed safely when water, power, or stores are unreliable.

## You need
- Ready-to-feed formula (best), powdered formula
- Safe water (bottled or boiled), clean bottles, cups, soap
- Cooler, manual breast pump

## Steps
1. Breastfeed if possible and keep going; it protects against infection and needs no clean water.
2. Store ready-to-feed liquid formula — no water needed.
3. For powdered formula, use bottled or boiled-and-cooled water and follow the label's ratio exactly. Never dilute to stretch formula.
4. Wash and sanitize bottles; discard leftover formula after a feeding (or within the time on the label).

## Caution
Do not use homemade formula, plain cow's milk for babies under 12 months, or watered-down formula — these can cause serious illness. If a baby has fewer wet diapers, is very sleepy, or won't feed, get help.

## Field note
If bottles can't be sanitized, a small clean cup is easier to wash and many relief agencies recommend cup feeding in emergencies.
