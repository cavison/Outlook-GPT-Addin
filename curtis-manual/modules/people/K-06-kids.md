Help children feel safe and useful during a long outage.

## You need
- Books, cards, board games, art supplies
- Simple jobs list

## Steps
1. Get down at their eye level. Speak calmly, briefly, and truthfully.
2. Ask what they have heard or are worried about, and answer that question.
3. Give them a real job (water runner, flashlight checker, garden helper).
4. Keep routines, play, outdoor time, and learning.

## Caution
Watch for changes lasting more than a few weeks — trouble sleeping, regression, withdrawal, aggression — and get help from a doctor or counselor when possible.

## Field note
Children often take their cues from adults' faces; limit adult worry talk and news within earshot. A 'boredom box' of new activities to open one per day stretches a stash.
