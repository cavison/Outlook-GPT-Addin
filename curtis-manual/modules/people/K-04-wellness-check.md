Check every household on the block daily so no one is found too late.

## You need
- Block map and household list ([[K-02]]), log sheet
- Flashlight, whistle, a buddy

## Steps
1. Use the block list; start with the most vulnerable.
2. Work in pairs on the same route at the same time each day.
3. Ask: Warm enough? Water? Food? Medicines? Is your heat source safe (CO alarm)?
4. Log answers; report problems at the meeting or to 911 if urgent.

## Caution
If no one answers at a house where someone vulnerable lives, don't enter alone — go with a buddy and call 911 if possible. Suspect carbon monoxide if multiple people are sick or unresponsive; get out and call for help.

## Field note
Agree on a window signal (a card in the window: green = OK, red = need help) to make checks faster in deep snow or bad weather.
