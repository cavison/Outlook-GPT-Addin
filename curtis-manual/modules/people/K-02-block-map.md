Make a simple map of the households, needs, hazards, and resources on your street.

## You need
- Paper, pencil, clipboard; colored markers
- Info from [[K-01]]

## Steps
1. Sketch the street and number every house.
2. For each house list people, special needs, and skills.
3. Mark hazards: downed lines, gas meters and shut-offs, damaged trees.
4. Mark resources: generators, wells, chainsaws, first-aid skills, radios.
5. Make at least 2 copies held by different people; update weekly.

## Caution
Treat downed lines as live. Never shut off natural gas unless you smell gas or hear a leak — only the utility (in much of Ottawa County, Consumers Energy or DTE) can safely turn it back on.

## Field note
The 'Map Your Neighborhood' program (developed by Washington State Emergency Management Division and used by some Michigan emergency managers) uses this same approach — ask county emergency management for the free guide.
