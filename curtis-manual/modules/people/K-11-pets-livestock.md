Keep pets, backyard chickens, and small livestock fed, watered, and sheltered through a long outage.

## You need
- 90-day supply of feed/pet food in sealed bins
- Water containers, heated-base backup (or ice-breaking tool)
- Vet records, medicines, ID tags/microchip info, carriers, leashes

## Steps
1. Store feed, medicine, vet records, and photos; make sure every pet has ID.
2. Give fresh water daily; in winter, break ice at least twice a day.
3. Provide shelter from wind and cold (dry bedding, draft-free coop) and from summer heat (shade, airflow).
4. Plan for evacuation: carriers, leashes, and a list of pet-friendly shelters or friends.

## Caution
Never bring a generator, propane heater, or grill into a barn or coop — fire and CO risk. Heat lamps in coops are a common cause of barn fires.

## Field note
Animals need more food in cold weather to stay warm. Many Ottawa County townships restrict backyard livestock — check local ordinances and Michigan's Right to Farm (GAAMPs) rules.
