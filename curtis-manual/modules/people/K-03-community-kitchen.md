Pool food, fuel, and power so neighbors can eat hot meals and charge phones and devices.

## You need
- Host with a generator or solar, plus a covered outdoor space
- Outdoor stoves/grills, handwashing station, food thermometer
- Power strips, sign-up sheet, tape for labels

## Steps
1. Choose a host with power and a covered outdoor area; agree on hours.
2. Set up a handwashing station and post simple food-safety rules (cook to safe temps, keep cold food at 40°F or below).
3. Cook only outdoors with fuel-burning stoves and grills, well away from doors, windows, and vents.
4. Run a charging table with a sign-up sheet and time slots; label cords and devices with names.

## Caution
Never run grills, camp stoves, or generators indoors or in garages — carbon monoxide kills. Medical devices get charging priority.

## Field note
A shared meal once a day saves fuel compared with 10 houses each cooking, and doubles as the daily check-in. A cheap food thermometer prevents a food-poisoning outbreak.
