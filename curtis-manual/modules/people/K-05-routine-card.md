Post a simple daily schedule so the family knows what happens when — routine lowers stress.

## You need
- Card stock or paper, marker, tape

## Steps
1. Set the same wake and sleep times every day.
2. Put meals and medicine times on the card.
3. Post chores by name: water, fuel, trash, dishes, wellness check.
4. Add a dusk safety check: CO alarms working, candles out or attended, doors and heaters safe.

## Caution
Fatigue causes accidents with chainsaws, stoves, and ladders. Schedule rest and no risky work after dark.

## Field note
Use daylight: in a Michigan December, the sun is up only about 9 hours — plan heavy chores and cooking before dark.
