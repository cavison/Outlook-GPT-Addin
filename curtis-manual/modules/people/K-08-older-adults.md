Protect older adults from cold, falls, dehydration, and missed medicines during an outage.

## You need
- Blankets, hats, warm layers; water at the bedside
- Medicine list and pill organizer; flashlight/headlamp

## Steps
1. Move them to the warmest safe room; hats and layers, especially at night.
2. Keep water and medicines within reach; track every dose on paper.
3. Prevent falls: headlamp or nightlight, clear paths, no throw rugs.
4. Check at least twice daily for confusion, unusual sleepiness, shivering, or signs of dehydration.

## Caution
Older adults can develop hypothermia indoors at temperatures that feel only cool to younger people. New confusion is a red flag for hypothermia, infection, or dehydration — seek help.

## Field note
Keep spare eyeglasses, hearing aid batteries, and denture supplies — losing them in the dark is common and isolating.
