Support a family member or neighbor who is overwhelmed, using Psychological First Aid principles.

## You need
- A quiet place, water, a blanket

## Steps
1. Make sure the person is safe and basic needs (warmth, water, food) are met.
2. Help them calm down: slow breathing, a quiet spot, a warm drink.
3. Listen without forcing them to talk about what happened.
4. Connect them to people and useful tasks, and to practical help.

## Caution
If someone talks about suicide or harming others, stay with them and get help now: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911.

## Field note
Psychological First Aid stresses five aims: sense of safety, calm, connectedness, self-efficacy, and hope. Giving someone a job is often more helpful than reassurance.
