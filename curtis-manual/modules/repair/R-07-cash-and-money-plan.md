Have cash and account information ready for when card readers and ATMs are down, and protect your money during a long outage.

## You need
- Cash in small bills ($1, $5, $10, $20) and some coins
- Fireproof, waterproof bag or small safe
- Copies of account numbers, insurance policies, and contact numbers
- FEMA/CFPB Emergency Financial First Aid Kit (free download)

## Steps
1. Keep a cash reserve at home in small bills; decide the amount based on your household budget.
2. Store it hidden in more than one place, in a fireproof/waterproof container.
3. Write down account numbers, insurer phone numbers and policy numbers on paper.
4. Spend cash only on needs; keep a simple ledger.
5. Keep money in an FDIC-insured bank or NCUA-insured credit union; insured deposits are protected.
6. Photograph damage and keep receipts for insurance or assistance claims.

## Caution
Don't keep all cash in one place or tell people how much you have; and don't pull all your savings out of the bank in a panic. Watch for scams pretending to be FEMA, utilities or contractors asking for cash up front.

## Field note
FEMA and the CFPB publish a free "Emergency Financial First Aid Kit" (EFFAK) checklist; filling it out once gathers every account, policy and document you'd need to restart life after a disaster.
