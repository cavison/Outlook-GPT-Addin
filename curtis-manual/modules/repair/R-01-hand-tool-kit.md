Gather the non-electric tools that handle most repairs when cordless batteries are dead.

## You need
- Claw hammer, handsaw, hacksaw with spare blades
- Screwdrivers (Phillips/flat) and a multi-bit driver
- Adjustable wrenches, pliers, locking pliers
- Tape measure, utility knife, pry bar
- Duct tape, zip ties, wire, assorted screws and nails
- Bucket or toolbox, headlamp, work gloves, safety glasses

## Steps
1. Lay out what you own; fill the gaps on the list.
2. Add a hand drill or brace if most of your drilling depends on cordless tools.
3. Put fasteners and tape in labeled containers.
4. Store everything in one bucket or box by the door to the garage or basement.
5. Oil metal tools lightly to prevent rust, and sharpen saws and knives.
6. Practice one repair with hand tools only before you need it.

## Caution
Wear safety glasses and gloves. Cuts and eye injuries are harder to treat when clinics are closed or overloaded.

## Field note
Keep a pencil and a small notebook in the kit to record what fasteners and parts you use. In week 3 of an outage, that list tells you what to trade for or scavenge.
