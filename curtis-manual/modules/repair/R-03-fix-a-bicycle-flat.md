Patch or replace a bicycle inner tube so your bike stays your fuel-free transport.

## You need
- Tire levers (2–3)
- Patch kit or spare tube in the right size (printed on the tire sidewall)
- Pump that fits your valve (Presta or Schrader)
- Wrench if wheel isn't quick-release

## Steps
1. Shift to the smallest rear cog, open the brake if needed, and remove the wheel.
2. Hook tire levers under the bead and pull one side of the tire off the rim.
3. Pull out the tube, pump a little air in, and find the leak by sound or in a bucket of water.
4. Run fingers carefully inside the tire to find and remove the thorn or glass.
5. Roughen the area, apply glue, let it get tacky, press the patch firmly (or use a new tube).
6. Put the tube back slightly inflated, tuck the tire bead in by hand, and pump to the pressure on the sidewall.

## Caution
If you skip step 4, the same thorn will puncture your new tube right away.

## Field note
Line up the tire's label with the valve hole when you mount it; next time you can find the cause of a flat fast, because the hole in the tube points to the same spot on the tire.
