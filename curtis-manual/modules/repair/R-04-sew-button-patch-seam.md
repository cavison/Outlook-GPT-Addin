Keep clothes, gloves and blankets working through a long outage with three basic hand-sewing fixes.

## You need
- Needles, heavy thread (button/upholstery thread lasts longer)
- Scissors, pins
- Fabric scraps for patches, spare buttons
- Thimble

## Steps
1. Thread the needle, double the thread and knot the end.
2. Button: sew through the holes 6–8 times over a pin or matchstick to leave slack, then wrap thread under the button to make a shank and knot it.
3. Seam: turn the garment inside out and backstitch along the old line, with small even stitches.
4. Patch: cut a patch larger than the hole, fold edges under, pin it and whipstitch around the edge.
5. Knot off on the inside and trim the tail.
6. Fix small holes in winter gear fast, before they grow.

## Caution
Wet, torn gloves and coats in a Michigan winter can lead to frostbite. Repair and dry them before going back out.

## Field note
Dental floss works as very strong thread for heavy jobs like backpack straps, boots and tarps.
