Stop a leak or burst pipe quickly, especially after a freeze, to protect your house and your water supply.

## You need
- Know where your main water shutoff is (tag it)
- Bucket, towels, flashlight
- Self-fusing silicone repair tape or a pipe repair clamp
- Push-to-connect (push-fit) couplings that match pipe size and type

## Steps
1. Shut off the main water valve right away (and the water heater's power or gas if the leak is big).
2. Open the lowest faucet to drain pressure; catch water in a bucket.
3. Dry the pipe and find the exact split or pinhole.
4. Wrap self-fusing tape tightly, stretched, a few inches past both sides of the leak, or bolt a repair clamp over it.
5. For a cut-out section, use push-fit couplings rated for your pipe type, deburred and pushed fully to the depth mark.
6. Turn water back on slowly and watch for drips for several minutes.

## Caution
Water and electricity together kill. If water is near outlets, a panel or appliances, don't wade in; shut power at the panel only if you can do it from a dry spot.

## Field note
In a long Michigan freeze, a split pipe often doesn't leak until it thaws. Before warming a frozen house, find the main shutoff and have someone ready to close it, then look for drips as each area warms.
