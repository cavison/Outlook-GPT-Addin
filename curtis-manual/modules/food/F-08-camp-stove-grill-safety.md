Cook with propane, butane, white gas or charcoal without poisoning your family or starting a fire.

## You need
- Camp stove or grill and the correct fuel
- Battery carbon monoxide (CO) alarm on each floor
- ABC fire extinguisher
- Long lighter, heat-proof gloves

## Steps
1. Set up OUTDOORS only, in the open, away from doors, windows, vents and the overhang of the house.
2. Put the stove on a stable, non-flammable surface. Keep a 3-foot kid-and-pet-free zone.
3. Check propane hose and connections with soapy water: bubbles mean a leak. Shut off and fix.
4. Light the match or lighter first, THEN open the gas valve.
5. Never leave a lit stove or grill unattended. Keep the extinguisher nearby.
6. Turn off fuel at the tank when done. Store cylinders outdoors, upright, never in the house or basement.
7. Dispose of charcoal ashes only when cold, in a metal can with a lid.

## Caution
Never use a grill, charcoal, camp stove or generator inside a home, garage, tent, porch or basement, even with windows open. Carbon monoxide kills quietly; many winter storm deaths come from this (CDC).

## Field note
Charcoal briquettes stay hot enough to start a fire for a day or more, and smoldering charcoal brought inside still makes carbon monoxide. Douse with plenty of water and stir. In snow, set the stove on a board or paver so it does not sink and tip.
