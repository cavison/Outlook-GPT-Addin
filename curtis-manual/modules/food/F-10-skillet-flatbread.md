Make fresh bread from flour, water and salt in a dry skillet, with no oven, yeast or electricity.

## You need
- 2 cups all-purpose or whole-wheat flour
- About 3/4 cup clean water (disinfected or boiled)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon oil (optional; makes it softer)
- Skillet (cast iron is best), bowl, clean cloth

## Steps
1. Wash hands. Mix flour and salt in a bowl. Add oil if you have it.
2. Add water a little at a time, stirring, until a soft dough forms that does not stick to your hands.
3. Knead on a floured surface for about 5 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest 10–15 minutes.
4. Divide into 6–8 balls. Press or roll each flat, about 1/8 inch thick.
5. Heat the dry skillet over medium heat (stove, rocket stove, or coals).
6. Cook each piece 1–2 minutes per side until brown spots appear and it puffs.
7. Stack cooked bread in a cloth to keep soft. Eat within a day or two.

## Caution
Raw flour can carry E. coli (FDA, CDC). Do not let children eat raw dough, and wash hands and surfaces after handling it. Use only safe water for the dough.

## Field note
Add a spoonful of dry milk powder, cooked mashed beans, or leftover oatmeal to the dough to stretch flour and add protein. Keep a damp cloth over waiting dough in a dry, wood-heated Michigan house; it crusts over in minutes.
