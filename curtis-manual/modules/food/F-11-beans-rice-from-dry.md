Turn dry beans and rice into safe, filling meals while using as little water and fuel as possible.

## You need
- Dry beans and white rice
- Safe water (disinfected or boiled)
- Pot with lid; haybox if you have one
- Salt, oil and seasoning

## Steps
1. Sort beans on a plate; pick out stones and shriveled beans. Rinse.
2. Soak beans in 3 times their volume of safe water overnight (8–12 hours). Drain and rinse.
3. Cover beans with fresh water. Bring to a rolling boil and boil hard at least 10 minutes.
4. Simmer until soft (often 1–2 hours), or move the boiling pot into a haybox ([[F-06]]). Lentils and split peas need no soaking.
5. For rice: about 2 parts water to 1 part white rice. Boil, cover, lowest heat ~15–20 minutes (or haybox).
6. Season and serve together. Rice plus beans gives better-quality protein than either alone.
7. Cool leftovers fast and keep below 40°F, or eat within 2 hours.

## Caution
Undercooked red kidney beans can cause severe vomiting within hours; just a few raw beans can do it (FDA). Always boil soaked beans hard for at least 10 minutes. Cooked rice left warm can grow Bacillus cereus; reheating does not make it safe.

## Field note
Save the soak water for flushing a toilet or for the garden, not for drinking. In winter, the cold soak water can sit in an unheated room. Adding salt to the soak water (about 1 tablespoon per quart) helps beans cook softer.
