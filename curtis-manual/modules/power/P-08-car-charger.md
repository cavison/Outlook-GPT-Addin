Charge phones, radios and small batteries from your car without draining it or risking CO.

## You need
- USB car charger and cables
- Small plug-in inverter (12 V socket type) for laptops or battery chargers
- Car with fuel; jumper cables or jump pack as backup

## Steps
1. Move the car outside, out of the garage, with the tailpipe clear of snow.
2. Start the engine before plugging in. Charge while it runs.
3. Charge everything in one session: phones, power banks, radios, battery chargers.
4. Keep plug-in inverter loads small; most 12 V sockets are fused for a modest load (check your owner's manual).
5. Stay in or near the car with a window cracked; do not sleep in a running car.
6. Unplug before you turn the engine off.
7. Note fuel used. Car fuel is also your way out; do not burn it down to empty.

## Caution
Never run a car in a garage, even with the door open. Snow packed around the tailpipe can push carbon monoxide into the cabin — clear it first and check after each snowfall.

## Field note
Hybrids and some electric vehicles can supply power for much longer and more quietly than idling a gas car, and some have a built-in outlet for small appliances. Read your owner's manual for "power outlet" or "V2L" before you need it.
