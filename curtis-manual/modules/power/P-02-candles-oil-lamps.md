If you must use flame for light, cut the fire risk to a minimum.

## You need
- Candles in sturdy, non-tip holders (jar candles, wide bases)
- Oil lamp with glass chimney; lamp oil labeled for indoor lamps
- Lighter or matches kept up high
- Smoke alarm, ABC extinguisher

## Steps
1. Use flashlights first ([[P-01]]). Use flame only if you run out of batteries.
2. Put candles in sturdy holders on a hard, flat surface.
3. Keep them at least 12 inches from anything that can burn: curtains, paper, bedding.
4. Keep them out of reach of children and pets.
5. Fill oil lamps only when cool, with lamp oil, outside or over a tray. Wipe spills.
6. Trim the wick short; a tall flame smokes and blackens the chimney.
7. Blow out every flame before you leave the room or go to sleep.

## Caution
Never use gasoline, camp fuel or kerosene not meant for the lamp. Never carry a lit candle while walking around, especially to light the way at night.

## Field note
Fire departments commonly report a jump in candle fires during power outages. Make "flame out before bed" one person's job each night, and keep a battery light by the bed so no one goes looking for matches in the dark.
