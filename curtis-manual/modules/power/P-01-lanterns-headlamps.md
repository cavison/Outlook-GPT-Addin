Light your home safely with battery LEDs instead of flames.

## You need
- One headlamp per person
- One or two LED lanterns for the shared room
- Flashlights, spare batteries ([[P-03]])
- Clear plastic gallon water jug (for the jug-lantern trick)

## Steps
1. Give every person their own headlamp and a fixed spot to keep it (bedside).
2. Use one lantern for the shared room. Hang it high so it lights more space.
3. Make a jug lantern: strap a headlamp to a full gallon water jug, light pointing into the jug. The water spreads the light around the room.
4. Use low or red mode when you can. It saves batteries and keeps night vision.
5. Turn lights off when you leave a room. Make one person the "light keeper" for the evening.
6. Recharge rechargeable lights during the day from a power station or car ([[P-07]], [[P-08]]).

## Caution
Use battery lights, not candles, whenever you can. Flashlights do not start fires, do not make carbon monoxide and are safe around kids.

## Field note
Headlamps on red light let you get up at night without waking the whole room or ruining everyone's dark-adapted eyes. Many cheap headlamps have it; check before you buy.
