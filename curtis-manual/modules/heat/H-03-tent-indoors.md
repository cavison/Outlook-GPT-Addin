Set up a small tent inside your warm room so body heat stays in a much smaller space at night.

## You need
- Small camping tent (or a blanket fort over a table)
- Mattress, foam pads, or layers of blankets for the floor
- Sleeping bags and quilts
- Flashlight or headlamp

## Steps
1. Set up the tent in your warm room ([[H-01]]), away from any heater.
2. Put insulation under it first: cardboard, rugs, then pads or a mattress. The floor steals the most heat.
3. Use the smallest tent that fits your group. Less air means it warms faster.
4. Toss a blanket over the top of the tent for an extra layer, leaving the vents open.
5. Get in with dry clothes, hats and sleeping bags. Share space; bodies warm each other.
6. Air it out each morning and dry damp bedding during the day.

## Caution
Never put a heater, candle, lantern or stove inside or right next to a tent. Tent fabric burns fast and traps carbon monoxide. Keep infants on a firm, flat sleep surface with no loose bedding (AAP safe sleep).

## Field note
Leave the tent vents open even when it is bitterly cold. Breath moisture otherwise condenses on the walls and soaks the bedding, and wet bedding is colder than a cooler tent.
