Stay warm through the coldest part of the night without running a heater while you sleep.

## You need
- Insulation under you: foam pads, cardboard, rugs, extra mattress
- Sleeping bags, quilts, wool blankets
- Dry base layer, hat, wool socks
- Water bottle with a tight cap; a snack

## Steps
1. Insulate under you first. Two blankets under you often help more than two on top.
2. Change into dry sleep clothes. Daytime clothes hold sweat.
3. Wear a hat and dry socks.
4. Eat a snack with fat or protein and drink warm water before bed. Your body burns food for heat.
5. Use the bathroom before bed so you do not have to get up.
6. Fill a bottle with warm (not boiling) water, cap it tight, wrap it in a sock and put it near your core or feet.
7. Sleep close together in the warm room or in an indoor tent ([[H-03]]).

## Caution
Boiling water in a bottle can burst or leak and cause serious burns. Never use a hot water bottle or heavy blankets with infants; use a wearable blanket or sleep sack in a clear crib (AAP safe sleep).

## Field note
Fluff your sleeping bag or quilt for a few minutes before you get in. Loft is what traps warm air; a bag left squashed in a pile all day starts the night cold.
