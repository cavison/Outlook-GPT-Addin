Know when to fight a small fire and how to get everyone out when the lights are already off.

## You need
- ABC fire extinguisher (one per level, one near the kitchen and the heater)
- Battery smoke alarms in every bedroom, outside sleeping areas, every level
- Flashlight or headlamp and shoes at every bed
- Escape ladder for upstairs bedrooms if needed

## Steps
1. Plan two ways out of every room. Pick a meeting spot outside (mailbox, a tree).
2. Fight a fire only if it is small, you have a clear way out behind you, and everyone else is leaving.
3. Use PASS: Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side.
4. If the fire grows, leave. Close the door behind you.
5. In smoke, crawl low under it. Feel doors with the back of your hand; if hot, use the other way out.
6. Go to the meeting spot and stay out. Call 911 from outside.
7. Practice in the dark once with a flashlight, so kids know the route.

## Caution
Outages bring candles, heaters and generators, and more house fires. Never go back inside a burning house for pets or belongings.

## Field note
Keep a headlamp, not a handheld light, at each bed. It leaves both hands free to open windows, carry a child and feel along walls. Test smoke alarms monthly; battery alarms are the only ones working when the grid is down unless yours have battery backup.
