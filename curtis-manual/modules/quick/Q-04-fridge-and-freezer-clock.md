How long food stays safe in a closed fridge and freezer, and when to start moving or tossing it.

## You need
- Appliance thermometers (one in fridge, one in freezer)
- Coolers and ice or frozen gel packs
- Masking tape and marker

## Steps
1. Note the time the power went out.
2. Keep the doors closed. A closed fridge holds safe temperature about 4 hours; a full freezer about 48 hours, a half-full one about 24 hours.
3. Before 4 hours, move milk, meat, leftovers, and medicines that need cold into a cooler with ice.
4. Check temperatures with a thermometer, not by smell. Throw out perishable food that has been above 40°F for 2 hours or more.

## Caution
Never taste food to see if it's safe. Food that has spoiled from bacteria often looks and smells normal.

## Field note
In a Michigan winter it's tempting to put food outside — but sun on a porch or a dark cooler can warm above 40°F even on a cold day, and animals find it. A cooler in an unheated garage or shaded north side, checked with a thermometer, is safer. Frozen jugs of water in the freezer now buy you hours later and become drinking water as they thaw.
