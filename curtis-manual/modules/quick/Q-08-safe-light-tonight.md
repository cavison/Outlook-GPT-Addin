Light the house after dark without starting a fire.

## You need
- LED flashlights and headlamps, one per person
- Spare batteries
- A gallon jug of water (for the lantern trick)
- Glow sticks for kids and night-lights

## Steps
1. Give each person their own flashlight or headlamp. Put it in the same place every night.
2. Make a room lantern: strap a headlamp to a gallon jug of water with the light pointing into the jug. The water spreads the light.
3. Skip candles if you can. If you must use one, put it in a sturdy holder on a clear, hard surface, and never leave it burning when you leave the room or sleep.
4. Use battery lanterns in shared rooms and keep spare batteries in one known box.
5. Turn lights off when you leave a room — batteries are your fuel ([[P-03]]).

## Caution
Candles start house fires during outages every year. Never use them near curtains, bedding, or children, and never to look for a gas leak or into a fuel tank.

## Field note
A headlamp on its red-light setting lets you move around at night without ruining everyone's night vision or draining batteries as fast. Lithium AA batteries handle Michigan cold far better than alkaline — keep those in a car or garage kit.
