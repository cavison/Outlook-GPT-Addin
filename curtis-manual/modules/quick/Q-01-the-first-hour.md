What to do in the first hour after the power goes out, before you know how long it will last.

## You need
- Flashlight or headlamp (not candles)
- Phone, charged if possible
- Battery or crank radio
- Clean containers for water
- Your paper plan ([[A04]])

## Steps
1. Get a light and check everyone in the house. Treat injuries first.
2. Look outside: is it just you or the whole street? Report the outage to your utility (Consumers Energy: 800-477-5050) and stay away from any downed line.
3. If you have city water, start filling clean containers and a bathtub now ([[Q-03]]).
4. Keep the fridge and freezer shut. Note the time the power went off ([[Q-04]]).
5. Unplug electronics and turn off big loads (furnace, well pump, water heater) so the surge when power returns doesn't damage them. Leave one lamp switched on.
6. Test your battery CO alarms. Do not run any generator, grill, or camp stove indoors.

## Caution
Downed wires can be live even when they look dead and even when your house has no power. Stay at least 25 feet away and keep kids and pets back.

## Field note
Write the outage start time on masking tape stuck to the fridge door. Days later, when everyone is tired and arguing about whether the chicken is safe, that piece of tape settles it.
