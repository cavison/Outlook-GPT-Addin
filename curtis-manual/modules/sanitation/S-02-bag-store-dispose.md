Safely seal, store and finally get rid of bagged human waste until trash pickup or sewer service returns.

## You need
- Heavy trash bags and twist ties
- Lidded outdoor trash can or tote (metal is best), labeled HUMAN WASTE
- Disposable gloves
- Cover material (sawdust, litter)

## Steps
1. Wear gloves. Tie the inner bag closed, squeeze out air, then tie the outer bag.
2. Put the double-bagged waste in a lidded can or tote marked HUMAN WASTE.
3. Keep the can outside in shade, away from the house door, wells, gardens and play areas. Strap the lid.
4. Sprinkle a little cover material between bags to cut odor and absorb leaks.
5. Follow local health department or trash hauler instructions for final disposal; they may set up special collection.
6. Wash hands after every handling.

## Caution
Do not pour bagged waste into storm drains, creeks, ditches or Lake Michigan tributaries, and do not flush toilets if sewers are backed up or your lift station is down. Do not burn plastic bags of waste.

## Field note
Many west Michigan homes on a city sewer still rely on electric lift stations; when power is out for long, the city may ask residents not to flush. Listen for that notice. If you have a septic system, toilets can usually still be flushed by pouring a bucket of water into the bowl, as long as the septic pump (if any) is not electric.
