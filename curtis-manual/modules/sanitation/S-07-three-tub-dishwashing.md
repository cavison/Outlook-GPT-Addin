Wash, rinse and sanitize dishes with a small amount of water so they do not make the family sick.

## You need
- Three tubs or basins
- Dish soap; scraper; scrub brush
- Unscented household bleach
- Safe water, hot if possible
- Rack or clean towel to air-dry

## Steps
1. Scrape food scraps into the trash first. Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel.
2. Tub 1 WASH: hot soapy water. Scrub cleanest items (glasses) first, greasy pots last.
3. Tub 2 RINSE: clean water. Dip each item to remove soap.
4. Tub 3 SANITIZE: 1 tablespoon unscented bleach per gallon of water. Soak each item at least 1 minute.
5. Air-dry on a rack. Do not towel-dry; towels re-contaminate.
6. Change the wash water when it gets greasy or cool. Use dirty water to flush toilets.

## Caution
Never mix bleach with ammonia, vinegar or other cleaners; it releases toxic gas. Mix fresh sanitizer each day, as bleach weakens over time. Do not use scented or "splash-less" bleach for food surfaces.

## Field note
Cut dish water by using fewer dishes: one marked cup per person per day, eating from the cooking pot, and lining plates with foil or wax paper. Wash dishes in daylight when you can see grease left behind.
