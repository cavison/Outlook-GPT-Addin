Stay clean, prevent skin infections and feel human using about a quart of water instead of a shower.

## You need
- About 1 quart of water (warm if possible), in a basin or bowl
- Two washcloths (one soapy, one rinse) and a towel
- Mild soap or no-rinse body wash
- Small cup or second bowl for rinse water

## Steps
1. Warm the room or wash in your warm room. Lay the towel down.
2. Split the water: about 2/3 in a basin with a little soap, 1/3 in a cup for rinsing.
3. Wash face first, then work downward: neck, arms, armpits, chest, back, legs, feet.
4. Wash the groin and bottom LAST (front to back), then do not reuse that cloth on the face.
5. Wipe off soap with the rinse cloth; dry well, especially skin folds and between toes.
6. Hang cloths to dry; wash them in hot soapy water every day or two.
7. Reuse the gray water to flush a toilet.

## Caution
Damp skin folds and wet feet grow fungus and rashes fast; dry completely. In cold houses, wash one area at a time and cover it again so you do not get chilled, especially elders and babies.

## Field note
Prioritize the "pits, bits and feet" every day and do a full sponge bath every few days to save water and fuel. Warm the water by setting a lidded jar on the wood stove or in the sun, not by boiling extra.
