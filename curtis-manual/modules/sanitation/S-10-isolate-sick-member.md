Care for someone with a contagious illness (vomiting, diarrhea, flu, cough) without the whole household getting sick.

## You need
- A separate room (or curtained corner) with a door or plastic sheet
- Their own toilet bucket, cup, plate, towel
- Masks, disposable gloves, trash bags
- Soap, water, unscented bleach, spray bottle
- Oral rehydration supplies ([[M-08]])

## Steps
1. Put the sick person in one room, near the warm room if possible, with the door closed and air flowing out a cracked window when weather allows.
2. Choose ONE healthy adult as caregiver. Others stay out.
3. Give them their own toilet bucket, dishes, cup and towel.
4. Caregiver wears a mask and gloves in the room; washes hands with soap after leaving.
5. Clean surfaces with 5 tablespoons (1/3 cup) unscented bleach per gallon of water, especially after vomit or diarrhea (CDC).
6. Keep them drinking: small sips of oral rehydration solution often. Log fluids, urine and temperature.
7. Know warning signs and when to get help ([[M-13]]).

## Caution
Dehydration from vomiting and diarrhea can become life-threatening quickly in babies, older adults and people with chronic illness. Watch for little or no urine, dry mouth, dizziness, or confusion, and seek help. Never mix bleach with other cleaners.

## Field note
Norovirus ("stomach flu") is not killed well by alcohol hand sanitizer; soap and water plus bleach on surfaces works (CDC). Put the sick person's laundry in a separate bag and wash it hot, apart from everyone else's.
