## Before (preparation)

- [ ] Store water: at least 1 gallon per person per day; aim for 14 days, plus a plan for 90 ([[W-13]], [[W-14]])
- [ ] Unscented 6% or 8.25% bleach and a dropper for water ([[W-04]])
- [ ] Battery CO alarm on every level and outside sleeping areas ([[Q-02]])
- [ ] Smoke alarms tested; fire extinguishers on each level ([[H-14]])
- [ ] 90-day pantry built and rotated ([[F-03]], [[F-04]], [[Z-03]])
- [ ] Safe cooking method that works outdoors without power ([[F-08]], [[F-05]])
- [ ] Warm room chosen; plastic, tape, blankets on hand ([[H-01]], [[H-02]])
- [ ] Winter sleep system for every person ([[H-10]])
- [ ] Lights: headlamp per person, lanterns, spare batteries ([[P-01]], [[P-03]])
- [ ] Generator (if owned) with transfer switch or cords; fuel stored safely ([[P-04]], [[P-06]], [[R-08]])
- [ ] Battery/hand-crank AM/FM and NOAA weather radio ([[C-01]])
- [ ] Family radio plan and frequency card ([[C-08]], [[C-04]])
- [ ] Two-bucket toilet kit: buckets, bags, sawdust or kitty litter ([[S-01]])
- [ ] First-aid kit and medication reserve ([[M-01]], [[M-10]])
- [ ] Plan for power-dependent medical devices ([[K-09]])
- [ ] Cash in small bills ([[R-07]])
- [ ] Go-bag per person ([[X-05]])
- [ ] Paper plan with contacts and meeting places ([[A04]], [[Z-04]])
- [ ] Know how to shut off water, gas, and main breaker
- [ ] Review insurance policy: flood, sump backup, food spoilage ([[V-05]])
- [ ] Meet neighbors; know who is vulnerable ([[K-02]])

## First 72 hours

- [ ] Do the First Hour card ([[Q-01]])
- [ ] Decide: short outage or long one? ([[Q-09]])
- [ ] Fill tubs and containers while water pressure lasts ([[Q-03]])
- [ ] Keep fridge and freezer closed; note the time power went out ([[Q-04]])
- [ ] Set up one warm room ([[Q-05]])
- [ ] CO alarms on; no grills, generators, or camp stoves indoors ([[Q-02]])
- [ ] Generator outdoors, 20+ ft from house, exhaust away ([[P-04]])
- [ ] Safe light only; limit candles ([[Q-08]])
- [ ] Get official news; ignore rumors ([[Q-06]], [[C-09]])
- [ ] Check on neighbors, especially elderly and medical-device users ([[Q-07]])
- [ ] Protect pipes from freezing ([[H-07]])
- [ ] Eat fridge food first, then freezer, then pantry ([[F-01]])
- [ ] Decide stay or go ([[Q-10]])

## Weekly

- [ ] Water budget: count stored gallons; refill / treat ([[W-14]])
- [ ] Fuel log: count gallons and propane; plan use ([[P-05]])
- [ ] Power budget: charge batteries and phones on a schedule ([[P-09]])
- [ ] Food inventory; adjust menu ([[F-03]])
- [ ] Test CO and smoke alarms
- [ ] Empty and clean toilet buckets; check waste storage ([[S-02]])
- [ ] Laundry and bathing round ([[S-05]], [[S-06]])
- [ ] Check every household member for wounds, illness, low mood ([[M-12]], [[K-07]])
- [ ] Street meeting / wellness check with neighbors ([[K-01]], [[K-04]])
- [ ] Check medication supply and expiration ([[M-09]])
- [ ] Inspect house: roof snow load, pipes, vents clear of snow

## Monthly

- [ ] Recount all food; recalculate days left ([[Z-03]])
- [ ] Plan food production: sprouts, microgreens, garden, fishing ([[F-12]], [[G-02]], [[G-01]])
- [ ] Service generator and small engines ([[R-02]])
- [ ] Rotate battery stock; check power station health ([[P-03]])
- [ ] Review security and community plans ([[X-03]], [[A18]])
- [ ] Review medical reserve; restock first-aid kit ([[M-01]])
- [ ] Family meeting: morale, routines, school ([[K-05]], [[R-11]])
- [ ] Update paper plan and contact list ([[Z-04]])

## After (power returns)

- [ ] Disconnect generator; restart in order ([[V-01]])
- [ ] Check for gas leaks and damage ([[V-02]])
- [ ] Sort food: keep, refreeze, toss; clean appliances ([[V-03]])
- [ ] Follow boil advisories; flush lines; test well ([[V-04]])
- [ ] Photograph damage; file claims ([[V-05]])
- [ ] Household after-action review ([[V-06]])
- [ ] Restock water, fuel, food, batteries, medicine ([[V-06]])
- [ ] Update this manual with your real usage numbers
