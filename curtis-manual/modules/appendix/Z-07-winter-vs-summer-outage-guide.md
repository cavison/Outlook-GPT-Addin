## A 90-day outage starting in January (west Michigan)
Planning model only. Real priorities depend on weather, your house and your household; follow official instructions when you hear them ([[C-12]]).

**Day 1 (first hours).** Note the start time ([[Q-01]]). CO safety first ([[Q-02]]). Fill water ([[Q-03]]). Pick and seal one warm room ([[Q-05]], [[H-01]]). Get reliable news ([[Q-06]]).

**Days 2–3.** Decide short vs long outage ([[Q-09]]) and stay vs go ([[Q-10]], [[X-06]]). Protect pipes or drain the house ([[H-07]], [[H-08]]). Move freezer food to outdoor cold storage with a thermometer ([[F-13]], [[Q-04]]). Check older and power-dependent neighbors ([[Q-07]], [[K-09]]).

**Week 1.** Set up the bucket toilet ([[S-01]], [[S-02]]). Start the daily routine card and wellness checks ([[K-05]], [[K-04]]). Run the generator safely if you have one ([[P-04]], [[P-05]]). Hold the first street meeting ([[K-01]], [[K-02]]). Start a listening schedule ([[C-12]]).

**Week 2.** Firewood and fuel tracking ([[H-06]], [[R-08]]). Insulate windows, try the tent-indoors trick ([[H-02]], [[H-03]]). Melt snow for washing water to stretch stored water ([[W-11]], [[W-14]]). Daily power budget ([[P-09]]).

**Weeks 3–4.** Expect fatigue and short tempers: stress first aid and kids' routines ([[K-07]], [[K-06]], [[R-11]]). Eat perishables in order and fight food fatigue ([[F-01]], [[F-16]]). Watch for hypothermia in older adults ([[H-11]], [[K-08]]). Laundry and hygiene ([[S-05]], [[S-06]]).

**Weeks 5–6 (February).** Deepest cold, short days. Ice safety before any ice fishing ([[G-13]]). Sprouts and microgreens for fresh food ([[F-12]], [[G-02]]). Medication reserve check ([[M-10]], [[M-09]]). Community kitchen to save fuel ([[K-03]]).

**Weeks 7–8.** Rotate and recount the pantry ([[F-03]], [[F-04]]). Repair and maintain tools and engines ([[R-01]], [[R-02]]). Barter carefully ([[R-06]], [[R-07]]). Keep security low-key ([[X-01]], [[X-03]]).

**Weeks 9–10 (March).** Thaw: watch for split pipes as the house warms ([[R-05]]), basement flooding and sump issues ([[W-13]]). Flies and odors return with thaw ([[S-08]]). Start seeds indoors and plan the bed ([[G-01]], [[G-04]], [[G-03]]).

**Weeks 11–13 (late March–early April).** Mud season: rain catchment once freezes end ([[W-10]]). Ticks may become active on warm days ([[M-15]]). Plant cold-tolerant crops when soil allows ([[G-01]], [[G-05]]). Prepare for restoration: restart sequence, gas check, food decisions ([[V-01]], [[V-02]], [[V-03]]), then review and restock ([[V-06]]).
