## First hours and safety
- Write the outage start time on masking tape on the fridge door; days later it settles the "is the chicken safe?" argument. [[Q-01]]
- CO alarms expire: check the "replace by" date now, and clear lake-effect drifts off furnace and water-heater vents. [[Q-02]]
- Close the water heater's cold inlet after filling the tub so it can't back-drain if pressure drops. [[Q-03]]
- An unresponsive person in a closed house with a generator or fuel heater may be a CO victim — get everyone outside first. [[M-07]]
- Make "flame out before bed" one person's job each night and keep a battery light by every bed. [[P-02]]
- Keep a headlamp, not a handheld light, at each bed so both hands are free in an emergency. [[H-14]]

## Water
- Bleach weakens with age: date the cap and replace stored bleach about once a year. [[W-04]]
- West Michigan is far below 6,500 ft, so a 1-minute rolling boil is always right here. [[W-05]]
- A filter that may have frozen should be replaced — keep it in the warm room at night. [[W-08]]
- Icicles from asphalt-shingle roofs pick up grit and chemicals; treat them like roof water. [[W-11]]
- Blue tape = drinking, red tape = flushing; it solves the 3 a.m. mix-up. [[W-13]]
- Put a full cup in front of each person; cold weather fools people into drinking less. [[W-14]]

## Heat and shelter
- The sunniest room at noon is often not the warmest at 3 a.m.; pick the room with the fewest outside walls. [[H-01]]
- Leave tent vents open in the cold; breath moisture otherwise soaks the bedding. [[H-03]]
- Blue can for kerosene, yellow for diesel, and label both — kerosene in a red can gets mixed up with gas. [[H-04]]
- An open fireplace can pull more warm air up the chimney than it gives back; heat only the area in front of it. [[H-05]]
- Many local sellers sell "face cords," not full cords — ask the piece length and do the math. [[H-06]]
- Test that your main water shutoff actually turns; old gate valves seize. [[H-07]]
- Hypothermia can happen above 40°F if someone is wet (CDC). [[H-11]]
- A split pipe often doesn't leak until it thaws — have someone at the shutoff as the house warms. [[R-05]]

## Food
- A coin on a cup of frozen water shows later whether the freezer thawed and refroze. [[F-02]]
- Hard-boil soaked red or white kidney beans at least 10 minutes before any slow or haybox cooking (FDA). [[F-06]]
- A Michigan garage swings from freezing to hot; store canned food on an inside wall instead. [[F-04]]
- Food left on a sunny porch or in a dark cooler can pass 40°F even on a cold day. [[Q-04]]
- A light-colored cooler on the north side holds steadier than a dark one in the sun. [[F-13]]
- Familiar flavors (spice blend, ketchup packets, chocolate) beat another bag of rice for morale. [[F-16]]
- Solar ovens rarely work in lake-effect winters; save them for late spring to early fall. [[F-07]]

## Growing, fishing and game
- Cold air drains downhill: plant tender crops on higher ground or against a south wall. [[G-01]]
- Cardboard over lawn in fall, covered with leaves, makes a no-till bed by spring. [[G-04]]
- Skinning and trimming fish fat lowers PCBs but not mercury. [[G-11]]
- After sewage overflows, fish upstream or in a separate lake until things settle. [[G-12]]
- New ice under fresh snow can be much thinner than nearby ice; honeycombed March ice fails suddenly. [[G-13]]
- Hudsonville is under 1,000 ft, so USDA base canning times apply with no altitude adjustment. [[G-18]]

## Power and light
- A "dead" cold battery often works again after warming in an inside pocket. [[P-03]]
- Size solar for December, not June, and stand panels nearly upright so snow slides off. [[P-07]]
- A generator doesn't need to run 24/7; run the fridge and freezer in blocks. [[P-05]]
- Clear snow from around the generator exhaust and intake — never move it closer to the house. [[P-04]]

## Communications
- Airplane mode between scheduled check-ins stretches a phone charge much further. [[C-02]]
- Always back up a Baofeng's existing memory before editing; it may hold calibration settings. [[C-03]]
- Write repeater offset direction and tone, not just the frequency. [[C-04]]
- At 2 m and 70 cm, height beats watts. [[C-06]]
- FRS/GMRS "privacy codes" hide nothing — treat every transmission as public. [[C-08]]
- AM travels farther at night; dial 2-1-1 for shelters and warming centers. [[Q-06]]
- A Baofeng cannot hear Michigan's digital public-safety radio (MPSCS). [[C-11]]

## Medical and health
- Gloves and gauze run out first; pack a small pocket kit in each coat. [[M-01]]
- Get a tetanus booster before an outage — 10 years, or 5 for a dirty wound. [[M-03]]
- Pen-mark the edge of redness around a wound to see if it is spreading. [[M-12]]
- A frozen insulin vial is ruined; never let it touch ice. [[M-09]]
- Alcohol hand sanitizer doesn't kill norovirus well; use soap, water and bleach (CDC). [[S-10]]
- Blacklegged tick nymphs are poppy-seed size; a daily full-body check is the real defense. [[M-15]]

## Sanitation
- Keep urine out of the poop bucket: less odor, lighter bags. [[S-01]]
- City sewer lift stations need power; listen for a "don't flush" notice. [[S-02]]
- Seal wet garbage within hours; flies breed in it in about a week of warm weather. [[S-08]]

## People, security and recovery
- Ask oxygen users now about their plan; backup tanks may last only hours. [[Q-07]]
- Many kicked-in doors fail at the frame — long screws in the strike plate are a cheap fix. [[X-02]]
- Give a stressed person a job; it often helps more than reassurance. [[K-07]]
- Put a printed contact list in every child's go-bag; phones die. [[X-05]]
- Dim or extra-bright lights on restore can mean a lost leg or bad neutral — shut off the main and call the utility. [[V-01]]
- Read your policy now: flood and sump backup are often excluded unless added. [[V-05]]
