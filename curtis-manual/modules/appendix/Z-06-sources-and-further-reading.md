The safety numbers in this manual come from the public agencies below. When guidance changes, they are right and this book is out of date. Download or print key pages **before** an outage — websites are useless when the power and internet are down.

## Government and official sources

| Source | What to use it for |
|---|---|
| **FEMA — Ready.gov** | Emergency kits, power outages, family plans, winter storms |
| **FEMA — DisasterAssistance.gov** | Applying for federal disaster aid |
| **CDC** (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) | Carbon monoxide, generator safety, water boiling, hypothermia, heat illness, hygiene |
| **EPA** (Environmental Protection Agency) | Emergency water disinfection with bleach; private wells |
| **USDA FSIS / FoodSafety.gov** | Food safety during and after outages |
| **USDA National Center for Home Food Preservation (NCHFP)** and the *USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning* | Canning, drying, smoking, fermenting — the only canning recipes to trust |
| **Dietary Guidelines for Americans** | Calorie needs by age and sex |
| **Michigan DNR** | Fishing and hunting licenses, seasons, regulations, ice safety |
| **MDHHS — Eat Safe Fish** | Which Michigan fish are safe to eat, and how often |
| **MSU Extension** | Michigan gardening, food preservation, well water, livestock |
| **Michigan EGLE** | Drinking water and well guidance |
| **NOAA / National Weather Service** | Weather alerts, NOAA Weather Radio |
| **FCC** | Radio licensing rules (Part 95 FRS/GMRS, Part 97 amateur) |

## Nonprofit and community sources

| Source | What to use it for |
|---|---|
| **American Red Cross** | First aid, CPR classes, shelters, disaster recovery |
| **ARRL** (American Radio Relay League) | Ham licensing study guides, emergency communications (ARES) |
| **Hesperian Health Guides — *Where There Is No Doctor*** (David Werner) | Medical care far from help; free online from Hesperian |
| **Hesperian — *Where There Is No Dentist*** (Murray Dickson) | Dental emergencies; free online from Hesperian |
| **WHO** | Oral rehydration guidance |
| **NFPA** | Home fire safety, extinguishers, alarms |

## Books (general reading)

- Matthew Stein, *When Technology Fails* — broad, practical self-reliance reference.
- Ted Koppel, *Lights Out* — journalism on why the U.S. grid is vulnerable to long outages.
