**Fill this in before you need it, in pencil, and verify every number once a year.** Phone numbers change. This manual only prints numbers that are national and stable; look up the rest from the organization's official website or your bills and write them in. Keep a copy in your go-bag ([[X-05]]) and paper plan ([[A04]]).

## Numbers you can trust

| Service | Number | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (police, fire, ambulance) | **911** | Life, fire, crime in progress, gas smell inside |
| Michigan 2-1-1 | **2-1-1** | Shelters, warming centers, food, help paying bills |
| Suicide & Crisis Lifeline | **988** | Mental-health crisis (call or text) |
| Poison Control | **1-800-222-1222** | Poisoning, CO questions, medicine mistakes |
