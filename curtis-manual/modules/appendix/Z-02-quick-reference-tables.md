The numbers you need most, on one page. Each table names its authority. If a table here ever disagrees with current guidance from that authority, follow the authority.

## Disinfect water with household bleach (EPA)

Use **unscented** liquid bleach with 6% or 8.25% sodium hypochlorite. Stir, then wait **30 minutes**. Water should smell faintly of chlorine; if not, repeat the dose and wait 15 more minutes. Double the dose for cloudy, colored, or very cold water (or filter it first).

| Water volume | 6% bleach | 8.25% bleach |
|---|---|---|
| 1 quart / 1 liter | 2 drops | 2 drops |
| 1 gallon | 8 drops | 6 drops |
| 2 gallons | 16 drops (1/4 tsp) | 12 drops (1/8 tsp) |
| 4 gallons | 1/3 tsp | 1/4 tsp |
| 8 gallons | 2/3 tsp | 1/2 tsp |

See [[W-04]].

## Boiling water (CDC)

| Elevation | Rolling boil time |
|---|---|
| Below 6,500 ft (all of Michigan) | 1 minute |
| Above 6,500 ft | 3 minutes |

Boiling kills germs. It does **not** remove chemicals, fuel, or metals. See [[W-05]].

## Fridge and freezer (USDA)

| Appliance | Holds safe temperature if door stays closed |
|---|---|
| Refrigerator | About 4 hours |
| Full freezer | About 48 hours |
| Half-full freezer | About 24 hours |

- Discard perishable food that was above **40°F for 2+ hours**.
- Frozen food with ice crystals or at 40°F or below can be refrozen.
- Never taste food to decide. When in doubt, throw it out.

See [[Q-04]], [[F-02]], [[V-03]].

## Carbon monoxide poisoning symptoms (CDC)

| Symptom | Notes |
|---|---|
| Headache | Most common |
| Dizziness, weakness | |
| Upset stomach, vomiting | Often mistaken for flu |
| Chest pain | |
| Confusion | |
| Sleepiness → unconsciousness | Sleeping people can die without waking |

**Clue:** several people (or pets) sick at once, better when away from the house. **Action:** get everyone outside into fresh air, then call 911. See [[Q-02]].

## Hypothermia stages (approximate core body temperature)

| Stage | Core temp | Signs | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mild | 90–95°F (32–35°C) | Shivering, clumsy hands, slurred speech, "umbles" (stumbles, mumbles, fumbles, grumbles) | Get dry, warm, sheltered; warm sweet drinks if alert |
| Moderate | 82–90°F (28–32°C) | Shivering slows or stops, confusion, drowsiness | Handle gently, insulate, warm the trunk; call 911 |
| Severe | Below 82°F (28°C) | Unconscious, very slow or no breathing/pulse | Call 911; CPR if no pulse; handle very gently |

Sources: CDC; Wilderness Medical Society. See [[H-11]].

## Oral rehydration solution (WHO home recipe)

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Clean (boiled or disinfected) water | 1 liter (about 1 quart + 2 tbsp) |
| Sugar | 6 level teaspoons |
| Salt | 1/2 level teaspoon |

Stir until dissolved. Sip small amounts often. Measure carefully — too much salt is harmful. Commercial ORS packets are better if you have them. Make a fresh batch every 24 hours. See [[M-08]].

## Daily calorie needs (approximate, Dietary Guidelines for Americans)

| Who | Calories per day |
|---|---|
| Children 2–8 | about 1,000–2,000 |
| Girls 9–18 | about 1,400–2,400 |
| Boys 9–18 | about 1,600–3,200 |
| Adult women | about 1,600–2,400 |
| Adult men | about 2,000–3,000 |

Lower end = sitting most of the day; upper end = active. Cold weather and hard physical work (hauling water, splitting wood) push needs higher. See [[Z-03]].
