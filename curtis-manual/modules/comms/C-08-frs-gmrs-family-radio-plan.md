Give every family member a simple walkie-talkie and a written plan for when and where to talk.

## You need
- FRS radios (no license) or GMRS radios (license, no exam)
- Batteries or chargers that fit them
- Pencil and a short written plan

## Steps
1. Choose FRS radios for no-license use, or get an FCC GMRS license (one license covers immediate family) for GMRS repeaters and higher power.
2. Pick a main channel and a backup channel; write both on each radio with tape.
3. If your radios use privacy codes (CTCSS/DCS), set them all the same; note that codes don't make talk private.
4. Set check-in windows (e.g., top of the hour for 5 minutes) and what to do if someone misses one.
5. Use first names or simple tags, short messages, and plain words. No addresses or "we have supplies" over the air.
6. Practice once with the family at the edges of your neighborhood to learn real range.

## Caution
Baofeng UV-5R class ham radios are not FCC-certified for FRS or GMRS. Use FRS or GMRS-certified radios for family-band use.

## Field note
FRS and GMRS share channels, and anyone can listen. "Privacy codes" only mute other people's traffic on YOUR radio; neighbors with codes off hear everything. Treat every transmission as public.
