Stretch your cell phone battery for days so you can text, get alerts and call 911 when a tower is working.

## You need
- Phone and charging cable
- Power bank (charged)
- Car charger or power station
- Paper list of key phone numbers

## Steps
1. Turn on Low Power / Battery Saver mode and dim the screen.
2. Close apps you don't need and turn off Bluetooth, Wi-Fi scanning and location unless needed.
3. Text instead of calling; texts often get through when calls fail on a busy or damaged network.
4. Keep Wireless Emergency Alerts turned on in settings.
5. Use airplane mode between check-ins, then turn it off at set times to check messages.
6. Keep the phone warm (inside a pocket) in winter; cold drains lithium batteries fast.
7. Recharge from a power bank, car or power station during the day, not overnight unattended.

## Caution
Never run a car in a garage to charge a phone, even with the door open; carbon monoxide builds up fast. Charge only with the car outdoors.

## Field note
When the tower has no signal, a phone keeps searching at full power and drains quickly. Airplane mode between scheduled check-ins can stretch a charge much further; you can still read saved maps and notes offline.
