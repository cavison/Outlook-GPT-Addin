Use simple window signs and a shared message board so neighbors and responders know who needs help without radios or phones.

## You need
- Cardboard or paper, thick marker
- Tape
- A whiteboard, corkboard or plywood sheet for the block
- Pencils and a box for notes

## Steps
1. Make two signs per house: a green "OK" and a red "HELP" (or just large words if no color).
2. Agree on the system with neighbors and post it on the block board.
3. Put the sign in a front window that faces the street each morning.
4. Set up a block message board at an agreed spot (a mailbox cluster or a porch).
5. Post date, time and name on every note; take down old notes daily.
6. Volunteers walk the street once or twice a day to check signs and the board.

## Caution
Don't post anything that tells strangers a house is empty, who lives alone, or what supplies you have. Keep notes to needs and check-ins.

## Field note
Many CERT teams use exactly this OK/HELP window-sign method after disasters because it speeds up door-to-door checks. A missing sign is a signal too: check on that house.
