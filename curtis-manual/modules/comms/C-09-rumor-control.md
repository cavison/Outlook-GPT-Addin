Sort true information from rumors before you drive somewhere, spend fuel or panic the household.

## You need
- Battery radio ([[C-01]])
- Notebook for a "rumor log"
- List of official sources (below)

## Steps
1. Ask: who said it, how do they know, and when?
2. Check an official source: NWS Grand Rapids, Ottawa County Emergency Management, your utility, Michigan 2-1-1, or local AM/FM news.
3. Look for two independent confirmations before acting on anything that costs fuel, money or safety.
4. Write the rumor, time, and what you found in your log.
5. Share only confirmed info, and say where it came from.
6. If you passed on something wrong, correct it quickly to the same people.

## Caution
Acting on a rumor ("the shelter has generators," "the water is poisoned") can strand you without fuel or make people sick. Confirm before you move.

## Field note
FEMA runs a "Rumor Response" page during major federal disasters. Locally, the county emergency management office and NWS Grand Rapids are the people actually making the calls; their posts and broadcasts beat a screenshot every time.
