Make a wallet-sized card listing every channel on your radio, your check-in schedule and callsigns, so anyone in the family can use it under stress.

## You need
- Pencil, index card, clear packing tape (to laminate)
- Current repeater list from RepeaterBook and your local club
- NWS NOAA Weather Radio station list

## Steps
1. List the NOAA weather channel(s) first; Grand Rapids area is 162.475 MHz (KIG63). Confirm on the NWS station list.
2. Add the national simplex calling channels: 146.520 MHz (2 m) and 446.000 MHz (70 cm).
3. Look up current local repeaters (frequency, offset, tone) on RepeaterBook and confirm with Ottawa County ARES/RACES or a local club.
4. Pick a family check-in schedule, such as the top of the hour for 5 minutes at set times, to save batteries.
5. Write channel numbers to match the radio's memory slots exactly.
6. Tape-laminate the card, keep one with each radio and one in the go-bag.

## Caution
Old repeater lists are often wrong: repeaters move, change tones or go off the air. Recheck your card every year and after any club announcement, or you may be calling into silence in an emergency.

## Field note
Write the offset direction and tone, not just the frequency. In most of the US, 2 m repeaters use a 600 kHz offset and 70 cm repeaters a 5 MHz offset, but the direction (+/−) and CTCSS tone vary by machine; without the right tone the repeater ignores you.
