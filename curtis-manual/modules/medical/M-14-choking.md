Clear a blocked airway in a conscious adult or child (over 1 year) who cannot breathe, cough, or speak.

> This is not a substitute for training or professional care. Take an American Red Cross first aid/CPR class and a Stop the Bleed class before you need them. If 911 or any medical service is reachable, use it.

## You need
- Your hands

## Steps
1. Ask, "Are you choking?" If they can cough forcefully or speak, encourage coughing. If they cannot breathe, cough, or speak, act.
2. Have someone call 911 (or call yourself on speaker).
3. Give 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand, with the person bent forward.
4. Give 5 abdominal thrusts: stand behind, fist just above the navel, other hand over it, quick inward-and-upward thrusts.
5. Repeat 5 and 5 until the object comes out or the person becomes unresponsive. If unresponsive, lower them to the floor and begin CPR ([[M-07]]); look in the mouth for the object before breaths.

## Caution
Infants under 1 year need a different method (back blows and chest thrusts with fingers, never abdominal thrusts). Pregnant or very large people get chest thrusts instead. Anyone who received abdominal thrusts should be checked by a medical professional.

## Field note
Alone and choking? Give yourself abdominal thrusts with your fist, or thrust your upper belly against a chair back or counter edge.
