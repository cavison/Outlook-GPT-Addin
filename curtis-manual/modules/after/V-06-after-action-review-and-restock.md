Within two weeks of power returning, sit down as a household, write down what worked and what didn't, and rebuild your supplies better than before.

## You need
- This manual and your fuel/water/power logs
- Paper and pen (or the Z-01 checklists)
- Shopping list and a calendar

## Steps
1. Hold a household meeting while memories are fresh (within 1–2 weeks). Everyone talks, including kids.
2. Ask three questions: What went well? What went badly? What will we change?
3. Compare logs to plans: how much water, fuel, food, and batteries did you really use per day?
4. Write a restock list. Replace what you used first, then fix the biggest gap you found.
5. Spread purchases over several weeks or months so you don't drain the budget (buy one extra item each shopping trip).
6. Service gear: run the generator dry or add stabilizer, recharge batteries and power stations, refill propane, wash water containers.
7. Update the paper plan, contact list, and calendar reminders. Thank the neighbors who helped.

## Caution
Don't skip the review because everyone is tired and relieved. The lessons fade within weeks, and the gaps will be the same next winter.

## Field note
Write your real daily usage numbers inside the front cover of this binder. Your own numbers from a real outage beat any chart in a book.
