Grow fresh greens indoors in any season using a shallow tray, a little soil, and a bright window.

## You need
- Shallow container with drain holes (takeout tray, cut milk jug, foil pan)
- 1–2 inches of potting mix or garden soil
- Seeds: radish, broccoli, kale, mustard, peas, sunflower (untreated seed)
- Spray bottle or cup, a cover (plastic lid or second tray)

## Steps
1. Poke drain holes in the tray and set it on a catch dish.
2. Fill with 1–2 inches of moist soil and press it flat.
3. Scatter seeds thickly so they almost touch; press them in lightly.
4. Cover for 2–4 days to keep dark and damp until most sprout.
5. Uncover and set in your sunniest window (south-facing is best); turn the tray daily.
6. Water from the bottom or mist so leaves stay dry.
7. Cut with scissors just above the soil at about 7–14 days, when the first leaves open.

## Caution
Use untreated food or sprouting seed. Seed sold for planting is often coated with fungicide (usually brightly colored) and should never be eaten. Throw out any tray with fuzzy gray mold or a sour smell.

## Field note
Fuzzy white hairs at the base of radish and broccoli seedlings are usually root hairs, not mold — they show up when the air is dry and disappear when misted. Mold looks like cobwebs spreading across the tops of seeds and soil and smells musty.
