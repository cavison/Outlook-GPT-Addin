Grow food in buckets and tubs on a porch, driveway or balcony — no digging needed.

## You need
- 5-gallon buckets or tubs (food-grade preferred; never ones that held chemicals)
- Drill or hot nail for drain holes
- Potting mix, or garden soil mixed with compost
- Gravel or a few sticks to raise the bucket off the ground
- Seeds or transplants

## Steps
1. Drill 5–8 holes in the bottom (or low on the sides) for drainage.
2. Raise the bucket on bricks or sticks so water can escape.
3. Fill to about 1 inch from the rim with loose potting mix and compost.
4. Plant one big crop (tomato, pepper, potatoes) or several small ones (lettuce, radishes, herbs, bush beans).
5. Place where it gets 6+ hours of direct sun.
6. Water when the top inch is dry — in summer heat that may be daily.
7. Feed every few weeks with compost, diluted urine (see Field note), or fertilizer.

## Caution
Do not use buckets that held paint, solvents, pesticides or motor oil. Containers dry out fast; a missed day in July heat can kill plants.

## Field note
Fresh human urine is mostly nitrogen and is generally free of disease germs from a healthy person. Diluted about 1 part to 10 parts water, it can be poured on soil (not leaves) around heavy feeders. Skip it on crops you will eat raw within a few weeks of harvest.
