Two hidden water reserves: the toilet tank (not the bowl) and the water sitting in your house pipes.

## You need
- Clean cup or ladle and clean containers
- Bucket
- Bleach or a way to boil ([[W-04]], [[W-05]])

## Steps
1. Toilet tank: lift the lid and look. If the water is clear and has never had a drop-in cleaner or color tablet, scoop it out into a clean container. Never use bowl water for drinking.
2. Pipes: shut off the main water valve where the line enters the house so contaminated water can't flow in.
3. Open the highest faucet in the house (upstairs sink) to let air in.
4. Open the lowest faucet (basement sink, laundry tub, or outdoor spigot) and catch the water.
5. Disinfect tank and pipe water before drinking ([[W-04]] or [[W-05]]).

## Caution
Tank water with blue, green, or bleach-type cleaning tablets is poisonous to drink — and boiling won't fix it. Use it only for flushing.

## Field note
Draining the pipes also helps protect them from freezing if your house goes cold ([[H-08]]). A typical house holds a few gallons in its lines — not a lot, but enough for a day of drinking water for a small family. Turn the main back on slowly when service returns and run cold taps until clear.
