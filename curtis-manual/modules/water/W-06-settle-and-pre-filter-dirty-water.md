Clear muddy or murky water so that bleach, boiling, or a filter can do its job.

## You need
- Two clean buckets or pots
- Coffee filters, a clean cotton T-shirt, or paper towels
- Ladle or cup

## Steps
1. Let the water sit undisturbed in a covered bucket for at least 30 minutes, longer (overnight) if very muddy, until sediment sinks.
2. Gently pour or ladle the clearer top water into a second clean container. Leave the sludge behind.
3. Pour that water through a coffee filter, several layers of clean cloth, or a paper towel.
4. Now disinfect it: boil ([[W-05]]) or use the cloudy-water bleach dose ([[W-04]]).

## Caution
Clear-looking water is not safe water. Settling and cloth filtering remove dirt, not germs — always disinfect afterward.

## Field note
A cloth filter works better if you fold an old cotton sari or T-shirt into several layers; research in Bangladesh found folded cloth filtering removed a meaningful share of cholera-carrying particles. It's a pre-filter only — but it also keeps sand and grit from clogging your good portable filter ([[W-08]]).
